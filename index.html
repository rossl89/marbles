1. MAKE YOUR PICKS

PICK ACROSS THE WEEKEND.

Formula 1, WEC, British GT, BTCC, MotoGP and more. Pick from the championships racing that weekend and score points in each competition. You can enter as many or as few as you like.

2. CHOOSE YOUR OVERALL PICKS

PICK YOUR BEST THREE.

From all the picks you make that weekend, choose up to three as your Overall picks. These are the picks that score towards your season-long MARBLES Overall Championship. You can only use one Overall pick from each real-world event.

Example:

You make picks in F1, WEC Hypercar, WEC LMGT3, British GT GT3 and MotoGP. You then choose your F1, WEC Hypercar and MotoGP picks as your three Overall picks.

3. HOW POINTS WORK

RESULT × MULTIPLIER.

Your finishing result gives you base points. Before the weekend starts, every competitor is given a MARBLES multiplier based on their expected performance. Favourites have a lower multiplier. Competitors who are less expected to perform well have a higher multiplier.

FINISH

P1 = 100 points

P2 = 85, P3 = 72, P4 = 62 and the points reduce further down the results.

MULTIPLIER

1.00× to 2.00×

The multiplier is set before the event. A harder successful prediction can therefore score more.

SCORE

Base points × multiplier

For example: 72 points × 1.60× = 115.2 MARBLES points.

4. EXAMPLE · SILVERSTONE 2025

YOU DON'T HAVE TO PICK THE WINNER.

Nico Hülkenberg started the 2025 British Grand Prix in 19th place and finished 3rd. A driver expected to struggle would receive a higher multiplier than one of the favourites. That means an unexpected podium can be a very valuable MARBLES pick.
